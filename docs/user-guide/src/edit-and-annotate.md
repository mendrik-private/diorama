# Edit and annotate

## Make reversible image changes

Use the editing tools to crop a selection, rotate, flip, scale, reduce colours,
or crop detected content. Diorama keeps an immutable source and an undoable
operation history during the session. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to
undo and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to redo.

## Point out visual details

Press <kbd>O</kbd> for a translucent highlight, <kbd>A</kbd> for an arrow, and
<kbd>T</kbd> for text. The palette controls colour, stroke size, and text size.
To reproduce the example: choose a contrasting colour, press <kbd>O</kbd>, and
drag out a highlight over the face or wing. Press <kbd>A</kbd> and drag an
arrow from its label toward the detail; drag the selected arrow's control
handle to curve it. Press <kbd>T</kbd> and drag in the label's direction, type
the label in the inline editor, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Drag the selected
text's bend handle to curve the text. Placed annotations can be selected,
moved, resized, bent, rotated, recoloured, or deleted.

[![Fantasy character annotated with a contrasting highlight on a wing, curved arrow to a face, and curved text](assets/screenshots/annotate-character.png)](assets/screenshots/annotate-character.png)

*Cyan marks the face and yellow marks the wing, with curved arrows and a
curved label. Select the image to view it at full size.*

Choose annotation colours that remain visible against the artwork. In
particular, do not place a red marker on a red surface; use a contrasting
colour for the highlighted area. Right-click samples a drawing colour from the
image. For pencil strokes, hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> for connected straight lines,
<kbd>Shift</kbd> for rectangles, or <kbd>Alt</kbd> for circles.
